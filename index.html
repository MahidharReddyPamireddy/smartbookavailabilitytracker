AI Demand Forecasting & Stockout Predictor

Local predictive algorithm based on 7-day checkout velocity, moving averages, and safety buffers.

🧠 Explainable Forecasting Engine (No Server or Cloud ML Required)

Our algorithm inspects sample 7-day daily checkout trends: Daily Average = (Day 1 + ... + Day 7) / 7 → Expected 7-Day Demand = Daily Average × 7. It compares predicted demand against currently available copies. If demand exceeds stock, it marks the book as High Stockout Risk and calculates a suggested restocking quantity with a 25% safety buffer.
